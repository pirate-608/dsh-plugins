# Finishing workflows

## Captions

Confirm language, transcription scope, caption format, maximum lines, and whether burned-in or sidecar delivery is required. Review names, numbers, punctuation, timing, reading speed, line breaks, and overlaps. Never describe auto-transcription as proofread without inspection.

## Audio

Preserve dialogue intelligibility first. Check track routing, clip gain, effects, peaks, fades, and music balance. Avoid applying destructive normalization or broad noise reduction without auditioning representative sections. Use meters and the user's loudness target when supplied; otherwise report the measured state rather than assuming a platform standard.

## Color

Identify input color space and any existing source, clip, adjustment-layer, or sequence transforms before adding corrections. Correct exposure and white balance before creative grading. Use scopes when available and check multiple representative shots. Avoid stacking duplicate LUTs or transforms.

## Quality check

Review the opening, closing, transitions, graphics, captions, loud sections, dark or bright shots, and any location where effects or speed changes occur. Record unresolved issues such as clipped audio, missing fonts, offline media, render warnings, or inconsistent color.
