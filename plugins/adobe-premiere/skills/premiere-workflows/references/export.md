# Export workflow

## Preflight

Verify the active sequence, in/out range, duration, frame size, frame rate, audio presence, captions, graphics, offline media, and render warnings. Confirm the requested platform or delivery specification and exact destination path.

## Configure

Choose container, codec, resolution, frame rate, field order, aspect ratio, bitrate mode, target/max bitrate, audio codec, sample rate, channels, loudness treatment, caption embedding or sidecar settings, and filename from the user's requirements. Do not silently change sequence or delivery frame rate.

## Commit point

Show or state the final filename, destination, format, resolution, frame rate, and key audio/caption settings before starting a long export. If the destination already exists, request action-time confirmation before replacement.

## Verify output

After export, confirm completion and file presence. When practical, open or import the rendered output and inspect the beginning, a middle section, the end, sync, audio, graphics, captions, framing, and visible artifacts. Distinguish successful encoding from completed quality control.
